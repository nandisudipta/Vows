## Principles (constant)

1. **Local-first**: Data stays on your machine unless you choose otherwise.
2. **Privacy-first**: No telemetry. No cloud dependencies. No data harvesting.
3. **Modular**: Every module is independent and swappable.
4. **Production-quality**: No demos. No shortcuts. Ship real software.
5. **Beautiful**: Apple-level design. Minimal. Fast. Intentional.
